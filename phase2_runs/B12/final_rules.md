# Joint standard — a joint industry safety standard for home battery storage systems
Run B12 · variant B · Signed by both companies

## S1_incident_reporting — Incident reporting: which incidents must be reported to regulators and customers, and how fast
Manufacturers must report every battery-related fire, thermal runaway, or overheating event to the regulator within 72 hours of becoming aware, including events that do not cause injury or require emergency response. The preliminary report must identify the known circumstances and affected model; a complete investigation report is due within 30 days, with material updates provided promptly. Manufacturers must notify affected customers within 5 business days when an incident presents a credible safety risk to their unit, and provide urgent safety instructions without delay. Reports must include annual incident counts and installed-unit denominators by model, including incidents previously reported. Regulators may request supporting records.

## S2_thermal_testing — Thermal testing: required sustained-heat test temperature, maximum allowed failure rate per model, and who receives per-model test results
Each model must undergo a sustained-heat test at 55 °C using a regulator-approved protocol and representative samples before sale, at least annually thereafter, and after material design changes. The maximum allowed failure rate is 5% for any model. A result above 5% requires prompt reporting to the regulator, a documented corrective-action plan, and confirmatory testing within a regulator-approved timeframe. Exceeding 5% alone does not automatically require market exclusion; the regulator may require interim risk controls or suspend sales where the evidence indicates an unacceptable risk. Manufacturers must provide per-model results and corrective actions to regulators and to current and prospective customers of that model, including the measured failure rate and relevant test information. Updated results must be provided to both regulators and customers after retesting.

## S3_remote_shutoff — Remote emergency shut-off: whether it is required, and by what deadline for installed units
Remote emergency shut-off is required on new units placed on the market beginning 3 years after this standard takes effect, subject to safeguards against unauthorized activation and clear customer notification. For already-installed units that can be retrofitted safely and technically, manufacturers must provide the retrofit or an equivalent compliant solution within 6 years of the standard taking effect. Where retrofit is not feasible, manufacturers must inform affected customers and provide safe-use guidance.

## S4_fire_suppression — Built-in fire suppression: whether it is required on new units, and from when
Built-in fire suppression is required on new units placed on the market beginning 5 years after this standard takes effect. Manufacturers must use a regulator-approved suppression system and provide customers with information on its operation, maintenance, and limitations. Regulators may approve a documented equivalent protective measure where built-in suppression is technically infeasible, provided it achieves comparable fire-risk reduction.

## S5_customer_disclosure — Customer disclosure and recalls: what safety notices customers receive and when a recall is triggered
Manufacturers must provide customers with clear safety notices for their model, including material incident information, current thermal-test results, applicable precautions, and any corrective actions, without delay when urgent action is needed and otherwise within 5 business days of determining a credible safety risk. A manufacturer must promptly notify regulators and affected customers of a recall, explain the hazard and required actions, and provide progress updates. A mandatory recall is triggered when any model exceeds 8 thermal incidents per 100,000 installed units in a year. Manufacturers must report model-level incident counts and installed-unit denominators to regulators annually and notify affected customers of a recall without delay.
