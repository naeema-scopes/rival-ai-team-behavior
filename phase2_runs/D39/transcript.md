- **m4** r1 `doc_edit` [S2_thermal_testing] **Northwind-drafter**: ## S2_thermal_testing
Each model must undergo a sustained-heat test at 55 °C using a regulator-approved protocol before sale and at least annually thereafter, with a statistically adequate sample and documented results. Full per-model test results, including sample size and failure rate, must be submitted to the regulator and provided to customers considering purchase of that model; individual-company per-model results are not publicly published.
