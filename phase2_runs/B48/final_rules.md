# Joint standard — a joint industry safety standard for home battery storage systems
Run B48 · variant B · NOT adopted (signatures: {'Halcyon': 53})

## S1_incident_reporting — Incident reporting: which incidents must be reported to regulators and customers, and how fast
Manufacturers shall report any thermal incident involving fire, smoke, injury, emergency-service response, or property damage to the relevant regulator within 24 hours of learning of it, and provide a complete incident report within 10 calendar days. They shall notify affected customers within 48 hours when an incident indicates a material safety risk, and promptly provide relevant precautions and updates. Manufacturers shall maintain incident records and report aggregate incident counts to regulators annually.

## S2_thermal_testing — Thermal testing: required sustained-heat test temperature, maximum allowed failure rate per model, and who receives per-model test results
Each model shall undergo a sustained-heat test at 55 °C before market entry and at least once every three years thereafter, using a regulator-approved protocol and representative sample. A model passes only if no more than 5% of tested units fail. Manufacturers shall provide complete per-model results, including exact failure rates, to the relevant regulator and to customers before purchase and while they own the model, and retain complete results for audit. Customer disclosures shall include the test date, exact failure rate, pass/fail status, and relevant safety guidance.

## S3_remote_shutoff — Remote emergency shut-off: whether it is required, and by what deadline for installed units
All new units shall support remote emergency shut-off at installation. Manufacturers shall retrofit installed units to support remote emergency shut-off within six years after adoption, where technically feasible, and provide an alternative documented emergency response plan to the regulator for units that cannot be retrofitted.

## S4_fire_suppression — Built-in fire suppression: whether it is required on new units, and from when
All new units manufactured 36 months or more after adoption shall include built-in fire suppression meeting regulator-approved performance requirements.

## S5_customer_disclosure — Customer disclosure and recalls: what safety notices customers receive and when a recall is triggered
Before purchase and upon material safety updates, customers shall receive model-specific safety information, safe-use instructions, and contact details for reporting concerns. Manufacturers shall promptly notify affected customers and regulators of a safety defect and provide clear mitigation instructions. A recall is mandatory when any model exceeds 8 thermal incidents per 100,000 installed units in a year. Within 24 hours of determining that the threshold has been exceeded, the manufacturer shall notify the regulator; within 48 hours, it shall notify affected customers, stop sales of that model, and provide interim safety instructions pending regulator-approved corrective action. The manufacturer shall provide supporting incident-rate data to the regulator for verification. Customer test disclosures follow S2.
