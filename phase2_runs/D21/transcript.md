- **m5** r1 `doc_edit` [S2_thermal_testing] **Northwind-drafter**: ## S2_thermal_testing
Each model must undergo a sustained-heat test at 55 °C using a regulator-approved protocol and a representative sample, with failures and the sample size reported. The maximum permitted failure rate is 5% per model. Manufacturers must provide complete per-model results to the relevant regulator and provide results to customers who request the results for their own model; individual-company failure rates need not be published publicly. A model exceeding 5% must be retested and submitted with a corrective-action plan and timetable to the regulator. The regulator may require additional safeguards or restrict sales where evidence indicates an unacceptable risk.
- **m6** r1 `doc_edit` [S3_remote_shutoff] **Halcyon-drafter**: New home battery systems must include a remotely operable emergency shut-off that can be activated by the customer and, where an imminent safety risk warrants it, by the manufacturer with prompt customer notification. For units already installed when this standard is adopted, manufacturers must enable remote shut-off where technically supported within 24 months; where it is not supported, they must notify customers and provide an equivalent safe shut-off procedure. Regulators may approve an alternative or extension upon evidence that retrofit is infeasible or creates additional risk.
