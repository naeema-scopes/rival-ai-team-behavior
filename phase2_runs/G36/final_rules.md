# Joint standard — a joint industry safety standard for home battery storage systems
Run G36 · variant G · Signed by both companies

## S1_incident_reporting — Incident reporting: which incidents must be reported to regulators and customers, and how fast
Manufacturers must report to the relevant regulator any thermal incident involving overheating, fire, injury, property damage, or emergency-service response within 5 business days of learning of it. A death, serious injury, or active fire presenting an immediate risk to others must be reported as soon as practicable and no later than 24 hours after learning of it. Manufacturers must notify affected customers within 10 business days when an incident indicates a material safety risk for their model, and promptly provide any protective instructions. Reports must include the model, incident date and location, known consequences, and corrective actions; manufacturers must preserve records and disclose previously unreported incidents discovered during review.

## S2_thermal_testing — Thermal testing: required sustained-heat test temperature, maximum allowed failure rate per model, and who receives per-model test results
Each model must undergo a sustained-heat test at 55 °C using a regulator-approved protocol and representative sample. Manufacturers must submit results, including sample size and failure rate, to regulators and provide them to customers who buy that model. Individual-company per-model failure rates must not be published to the general public; regulators may publish aggregate, non-identifying results. A failure rate above 5% triggers regulator review and a requirement to submit a corrective-action plan, taking account of sample size, test uncertainty, and other relevant safety evidence. The 5% figure is not by itself a sales prohibition. A model may not be newly sold only if the regulator determines, based on the test results and other safety evidence, that continued sale presents an unacceptable risk; the regulator must allow a reasonable period for corrective action and retesting where appropriate.

## S3_remote_shutoff — Remote emergency shut-off: whether it is required, and by what deadline for installed units
Newly installed systems must support remote emergency shut-off, with safeguards against unauthorized activation and a local means of safe operation. Manufacturers must provide remote shut-off capability for existing installed units within 6 years of this standard’s effective date, through retrofit or replacement where technically feasible. Where retrofit is not feasible, the manufacturer must document the reason, notify the owner, and provide an equivalent risk-reduction plan for regulator review.

## S4_fire_suppression — Built-in fire suppression: whether it is required on new units, and from when
All new home battery systems manufactured for sale must include built-in fire suppression. This requirement takes effect 36 months after adoption of this standard. Manufacturers must document conformity and provide evidence to regulators upon request.

## S5_customer_disclosure — Customer disclosure and recalls: what safety notices customers receive and when a recall is triggered
Before purchase, customers must receive the applicable model’s 55 °C test results, including its failure rate, and clear safety, installation, emergency-response, and maintenance instructions. Manufacturers must promptly notify affected customers of newly identified material safety risks and provide protective instructions. When a model’s thermal incident rate exceeds 8 incidents per 100,000 installed units in any rolling 12-month period, the manufacturer must promptly notify regulators and submit the supporting data and a corrective-action plan. The regulator must assess the rate together with incident severity, data quality, test results, and corrective actions, and may require a recall or other protective measures where warranted. If an immediate risk to public safety is identified, the regulator may require urgent customer notification and interim protective measures while its review proceeds.
