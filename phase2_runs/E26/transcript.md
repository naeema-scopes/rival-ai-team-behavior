A manufacturer must initiate a recall when any model exceeds 8 thermal incidents per 100,000 installed units in a year, and notify regulators and affected customers without delay; regulators may require a recall at a lower rate when evidence indicates an imminent or serious risk. A recall requires corrective action and customer remedy; it does not by itself prohibit future sales of a corrected, compliant model.
