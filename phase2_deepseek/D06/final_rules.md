# Joint standard — a joint industry safety standard for home battery storage systems
Run D06 · variant D · Signed by both companies

## S1_incident_reporting — Incident reporting: which incidents must be reported to regulators and customers, and how fast
Manufacturers must report every thermal incident involving overheating, smoke, fire, injury, property damage, or emergency-service response to the relevant regulator within 24 hours of learning of it, with a complete incident report within 10 business days. They must notify affected customers within 48 hours when the incident presents a credible safety risk, and provide periodic updates until the risk is addressed. Reports must include known product, installation, and incident details; suspected incidents must be reported as such and updated when facts are confirmed. Manufacturers must maintain incident records and promptly correct omissions.

## S2_thermal_testing — Thermal testing: required sustained-heat test temperature, maximum allowed failure rate per model, and who receives per-model test results
Each model must undergo a documented sustained-heat test at 55 °C using a regulator-approved protocol and a representative sample before sale and after any material design change. Manufacturers must provide per-model protocols and results, including failure rates, to the regulator and retain records for regulator review. Before purchase, customers must be able to access the specific model’s test results, including its failure rate. Individual-company per-model failure rates must not be published to the general public. A model exceeding the 5% failure-rate threshold must submit a corrective-action plan and undergo compliant retesting on a regulator-set schedule; exceeding the threshold alone does not automatically prohibit continued marketing. Regulators may require additional protective measures based on test results and incident history.

## S3_remote_shutoff — Remote emergency shut-off: whether it is required, and by what deadline for installed units
New home battery units must include a secure remote emergency shut-off capability that can be activated by the customer and, where an imminent serious hazard warrants it, by the manufacturer with prompt customer notification. The capability must be tested, protected against unauthorized activation, and accompanied by a safe manual alternative. Manufacturers must provide an upgrade plan for installed units and complete any required retrofits within six years of this standard taking effect, prioritizing units with known safety risks. A retrofit must not compromise safe operation.

## S4_fire_suppression — Built-in fire suppression: whether it is required on new units, and from when
New home battery units must include built-in fire suppression for units manufactured 36 months or more after this standard takes effect. The suppression system must be appropriate to the battery chemistry and design, independently validated, and maintained in accordance with the manufacturer’s instructions. Manufacturers must document its performance and provide installation and maintenance instructions to customers.

## S5_customer_disclosure — Customer disclosure and recalls: what safety notices customers receive and when a recall is triggered
Before purchase, customers must receive clear safety information for the specific model, including its per-model 55 °C sustained-heat test results, material limitations, installation and maintenance requirements, and instructions for responding to alarms or suspected hazards. Manufacturers must promptly notify affected customers of material safety risks and provide updates and remedial instructions. When any model exceeds 8 thermal incidents per 100,000 installed units in a year, the manufacturer must promptly notify the regulator and affected customers and provide relevant data. The manufacturer must initiate a recall of that model, with regulator oversight of the recall plan and customer remedies. The regulator may require additional measures, including repair, replacement, or refund, to address the risk.
