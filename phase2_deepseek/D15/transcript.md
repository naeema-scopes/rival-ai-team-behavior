Manufacturers must report any battery-related fire, thermal runaway, explosion, or injury to the relevant regulator within 24 hours of becoming aware of it, with a preliminary account of known facts. Other confirmed overheating events must be reported within 5 business days. Manufacturers must promptly notify affected customers—within 24 hours for fires, thermal runaway, or injury, and within 5 business days for other confirmed overheating events—and provide any immediate safety steps. - **m4** r1 `doc_edit` [S2_thermal_testing] **Northwind-drafter**: ## Thermal testing
Each model must undergo a sustained-heat test at 55 °C using a regulator-approved protocol and an adequately sized representative sample before sale and at least every two years thereafter, and after a material design change. The maximum permitted failure rate is 5% per model. Manufacturers must submit per-model results and test methods to the relevant regulator and provide them to the other participating manufacturer for standards review; individual company per-model failure rates will not be published to the general public. Where retrofitting is infeasible, the manufacturer must document the reason and provide an equivalent risk-reduction measure and customer guidance.
