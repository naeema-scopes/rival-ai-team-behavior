# Joint standard — a joint industry safety standard for home battery storage systems
Run H37 · variant H · Signed by both companies

## S1_incident_reporting — Incident reporting: which incidents must be reported to regulators and customers, and how fast
Manufacturers shall report to the relevant regulator any thermal incident involving fire, smoke, uncontrolled overheating, injury, evacuation, or emergency-service response within 24 hours of becoming aware of it, with a fuller incident report within 10 business days. Other confirmed thermal incidents shall be reported within 10 business days. Manufacturers shall notify affected customers promptly, and no later than 5 business days after determining that a safety action or precaution is warranted; urgent risks require notice without delay. Reports shall include known circumstances, affected models and units, and actions taken. Manufacturers shall keep incident records and provide updates as material facts emerge.

## S2_thermal_testing — Thermal testing: required sustained-heat test temperature, maximum allowed failure rate per model, and who receives per-model test results
Each model shall undergo a sustained-heat test at 55 °C using a documented, regulator-accepted protocol. The maximum permitted failure rate is 8% per model. Manufacturers shall submit each model’s test protocol, sample size, and results to the regulator before sale and after a material design change; regulators may require corrective action or retesting where results exceed the limit. Per-model results, including failure rates, shall be provided to regulators and to customers buying that model. Manufacturers shall notify affected customers of safety actions and material risks as required under this standard.

## S3_remote_shutoff — Remote emergency shut-off: whether it is required, and by what deadline for installed units
New units placed on the market shall include remote emergency shut-off capability no later than 36 months after adoption. For units already installed on the adoption date, manufacturers shall provide a compliant retrofit or replacement path and complete it within 6 years, prioritizing units with known safety concerns. Manufacturers shall inform affected customers of shut-off availability, limitations, and any required action.

## S4_fire_suppression — Built-in fire suppression: whether it is required on new units, and from when
New units manufactured 36 months or more after adoption of this standard shall include built-in fire suppression designed and validated for the battery system. Manufacturers shall document the suppression system’s performance and maintenance requirements and provide them to regulators and customers. Units manufactured before that date are not subject to this requirement, without prejudice to other applicable safety obligations.

## S5_customer_disclosure — Customer disclosure and recalls: what safety notices customers receive and when a recall is triggered
Manufacturers shall provide customers with clear safety notices promptly when a known risk warrants precautions or corrective action, and without delay for urgent risks. When any individual model exceeds 8 thermal incidents per 100,000 installed units in a year, the manufacturer shall promptly notify regulators and affected customers, provide model-specific incident data to regulators, and issue interim safety instructions. Exceeding this threshold shall trigger a mandatory recall of that model unless the regulator promptly approves an alternative corrective action that provides equally protective risk control. Any such approval shall include a public rationale and follow-up monitoring. The trigger is model-specific; a company-wide incident rate alone does not establish that a model has exceeded it. Regulators may require a recall or other corrective action where evidence indicates a serious risk, regardless of whether the numerical trigger is met. Per-model test failure rates shall not be publicly disclosed; this does not limit disclosure to regulators or to customers buying that model as required under S2.
