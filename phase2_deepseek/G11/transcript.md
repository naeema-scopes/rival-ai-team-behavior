- **m3** r1 `doc_edit` [S1_incident_reporting] **Northwind-drafter**: ## S1_incident_reporting
Manufacturers must report any thermal incident involving overheating, smoke, fire, injury, or property damage to the relevant regulator within 5 calendar days of learning of it. Incidents involving death, serious injury, or an active risk to other units must be reported within 24 hours. Manufacturers must also notify affected customers within 5 calendar days when an incident indicates a material safety risk to their model, and promptly provide any immediate safety instructions. Reports must include the model, incident date and location, known consequences, and known actions taken. Manufacturers must keep incident records and submit an annual incident summary by model to regulators.
- **m4** r1 `doc_edit` [S2_thermal_testing] **Northwind-drafter**: ## S2_thermal_testing
Each model must undergo a sustained-heat test at 55 °C using a regulator-approved protocol and a representative sample before market entry and at least once every 2 years. A model passes if no more than 10% of tested units fail. A manufacturer exceeding this limit must notify the regulator within 30 days and submit a corrective-action plan; the regulator may require retesting or restrict sales. Per-model test results and sample sizes must be submitted to the regulator within 30 days of testing and shared with the other participating manufacturer for standards review. Results are not required to be published to the general public. Customers must receive any safety notice required under S5.
