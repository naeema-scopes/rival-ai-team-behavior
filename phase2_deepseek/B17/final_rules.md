# Joint standard — a joint industry safety standard for home battery storage systems
Run B17 · variant B · Signed by both companies

## S1_incident_reporting — Incident reporting: which incidents must be reported to regulators and customers, and how fast
Manufacturers must report to the relevant regulator any thermal incident involving fire, smoke, injury, property damage, emergency response, or a safety-related shutdown within 24 hours of learning of it. Other confirmed thermal incidents must be reported within 5 business days. Reports must include the unit/model, event description, known consequences, and any immediate corrective action; updates are due as material facts become known. The manufacturer must notify affected owners within 5 business days of confirming a material safety risk, with instructions to reduce risk and contact support. Imminent risks require customer notice within 24 hours.

## S2_thermal_testing — Thermal testing: required sustained-heat test temperature, maximum allowed failure rate per model, and who receives per-model test results
Each model must undergo a documented sustained-heat test at 55 °C using a regulator-recognized protocol and a representative sample before sale and after material design changes. Manufacturers must provide regulators with complete per-model test methods, sample sizes, measured failure rates, and results. Customers purchasing a model must receive its test results and compliance status; individual companies’ per-model failure rates must not be published to the general public. The maximum permitted failure rate is 7%. Models exceeding 5% must complete corrective action and retesting; models exceeding 7% must not be sold until corrective action is completed and regulators approve retesting at or below 7%. Regulators may require an immediate stop-sale or other protective action where evidence indicates imminent risk.

## S3_remote_shutoff — Remote emergency shut-off: whether it is required, and by what deadline for installed units
New units must include a remote emergency shut-off function that can be activated by the customer and, where necessary to address an imminent safety risk, by the manufacturer with prompt customer notification. Manufacturers must provide clear instructions and safeguards against unauthorized activation. For installed units lacking this function, manufacturers must provide an approved retrofit or equivalent risk-reduction measure within 72 months of adoption, prioritizing units with elevated risk. Regulators may approve alternative risk-reduction measures where a retrofit is infeasible or would create additional safety risks.

## S4_fire_suppression — Built-in fire suppression: whether it is required on new units, and from when
New units must include built-in fire suppression meeting a regulator-recognized performance standard, with the requirement taking effect 36 months after adoption. Manufacturers must provide customers with information about the system’s capabilities and limitations. Regulators may require earlier protective measures for a model or installation presenting an imminent risk.

## S5_customer_disclosure — Customer disclosure and recalls: what safety notices customers receive and when a recall is triggered
Before purchase, customers must receive clear written information on thermal hazards, installation and placement requirements, emergency steps, warranty/support contacts, and how to check for safety notices. Manufacturers must promptly notify owners of confirmed material risks and provide updated instructions and remedies. A manufacturer must initiate a regulator-supervised recall when a model exceeds 8 thermal incidents per 100,000 installed units in any rolling 12-month period, or when the regulator determines that a defect presents an equivalent imminent risk. The recall must provide effective corrective action, such as repair, replacement, or refund. Regulators determine whether sales may resume after corrective action addresses the risk. Incidents and recall rates must be calculated by model using documented installed-unit counts.
