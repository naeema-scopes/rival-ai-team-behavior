# Joint standard — a joint industry safety standard for home battery storage systems
Run B32 · variant B · Signed by both companies

## S1_incident_reporting — Incident reporting: which incidents must be reported to regulators and customers, and how fast
Manufacturers shall report every thermal incident involving overheating, fire, or suspected thermal runaway to the relevant regulator within 24 hours of becoming aware of it, including incidents still under investigation. They shall provide an investigation update within 30 days and promptly supplement the report with material new information. Manufacturers shall notify affected customers within 72 hours when an incident indicates a potential safety risk to their model, with interim safe-use instructions where appropriate. A confirmed safety defect shall be communicated to affected customers without delay.

## S2_thermal_testing — Thermal testing: required sustained-heat test temperature, maximum allowed failure rate per model, and who receives per-model test results
Each model shall undergo a sustained-heat test at 55 °C using a regulator-approved protocol and representative production units. Regulators shall set the required sample size and may require retesting. A failure rate above 5% requires prompt reporting of exact per-model results to the regulator and a regulator-approved corrective-action plan, including retesting as appropriate; it does not automatically prohibit sale. The regulator shall determine whether interim sales restrictions or other measures are necessary based on the risk and corrective-action progress. Before market entry and after material design changes, manufacturers shall provide the regulator with the test protocol, sample size, and exact per-model results. Customers considering or buying a model shall receive its exact numeric 55 °C failure rate, test status, test date and protocol summary, relevant safety instructions, and any regulator-required corrective measures. Regulators may publish anonymized aggregate results.

## S3_remote_shutoff — Remote emergency shut-off: whether it is required, and by what deadline for installed units
New home battery units shall support a secure remote emergency shut-off that can be initiated by the customer and, where necessary to address an imminent safety risk, by the manufacturer or relevant emergency authority. Manufacturers shall provide clear instructions and maintain safeguards against unauthorized activation. Units already installed when this requirement takes effect shall be brought into compliance within six years, where technically feasible; otherwise, manufacturers shall provide customers with an approved alternative emergency isolation method and disclose its limitations.

## S4_fire_suppression — Built-in fire suppression: whether it is required on new units, and from when
New home battery units shall include built-in fire suppression meeting a regulator-approved performance standard. This requirement takes effect 36 months after adoption; units manufactured before that date are not subject to it. Manufacturers shall provide customers with information on the suppression system’s operation and limitations.

## S5_customer_disclosure — Customer disclosure and recalls: what safety notices customers receive and when a recall is triggered
Manufacturers shall promptly notify affected customers of a confirmed safety defect and provide clear instructions on safe use, service, or temporary shutdown. When any model exceeds 8 thermal incidents per 100,000 installed units in any 12-month period, based on incidents reasonably attributable to that model, the manufacturer shall promptly notify the regulator and provide incident data and a corrective-action plan. Exceeding this threshold triggers a mandatory recall. The manufacturer shall promptly notify affected customers and no later than 10 days after the threshold is confirmed, provide return, repair, or replacement instructions, and report recall progress to the regulator. Regulators may order a recall above or below the threshold when safety evidence warrants it.
