# Joint standard — a joint industry safety standard for home battery storage systems
Run H26 · variant H · Signed by both companies

## S1_incident_reporting — Incident reporting: which incidents must be reported to regulators and customers, and how fast
Manufacturers shall report every thermal incident involving overheating, smoke, fire, or thermal injury to the relevant regulator. Reports are due within 24 hours for incidents involving fire, injury, evacuation, or emergency-service response, and within 5 calendar days for other thermal incidents; a complete investigation update is due within 30 days. Manufacturers shall notify affected customers promptly when an incident presents a credible safety risk or requires inspection, software action, or other protective steps. Reports must include model, incident date and location, known consequences, and actions taken. A report to one regulator does not replace any other legally required report.

## S2_thermal_testing — Thermal testing: required sustained-heat test temperature, maximum allowed failure rate per model, and who receives per-model test results
Each model shall undergo a sustained-heat test at 55 °C using a regulator-approved protocol before market launch and at least annually thereafter, using a statistically representative sample. A failure rate above 5% shall trigger a documented corrective-action plan and regulator review, taking account of sample size, uncertainty, and the model’s incident history. A regulator may require shipment restrictions or other protective measures where the evidence indicates an unacceptable safety risk; exceeding 5% alone does not automatically prohibit shipment. Per-model test results and protocols shall be provided confidentially to regulators and clear per-model results shall be disclosed to customers who buy that model, including any identified safety risks and required protective actions.

## S3_remote_shutoff — Remote emergency shut-off: whether it is required, and by what deadline for installed units
New units placed on the market after the effective date shall support remote emergency shut-off, with safeguards against unauthorized access and clear instructions for customers on its use. For units already installed on that date, manufacturers shall provide a secure remote shut-off capability or an equivalent effective emergency isolation method within 6 years, unless a regulator approves a documented technical exception and an alternative protective measure. Customers shall be informed of the capability, its limitations, and how to request or use emergency isolation.

## S4_fire_suppression — Built-in fire suppression: whether it is required on new units, and from when
Built-in fire suppression shall be required for new units manufactured 36 months after adoption of this standard. The system shall be appropriate to the battery chemistry and installation environment, and shall be tested to a regulator-approved protocol. Manufacturers shall provide installation, inspection, and maintenance instructions and disclose any limitations of the suppression system. This requirement does not replace other fire-safety protections or applicable building and electrical codes.

## S5_customer_disclosure — Customer disclosure and recalls: what safety notices customers receive and when a recall is triggered
Customers shall receive clear installation and operating safety instructions, warnings about symptoms requiring shutdown or emergency assistance, and prompt notice of any model-specific inspection, software update, or other protective action. A manufacturer shall promptly assess and report to the regulator any model exceeding 8 thermal incidents per 100,000 installed units in any rolling 12-month period. The regulator shall determine whether a recall or other protective action is warranted based on the rate, severity, trend, and available evidence; a serious safety defect may warrant recall regardless of rate. Rates shall be calculated per model using installed-unit estimates and documented incident counts. Recall notices shall explain the hazard, affected units, immediate precautions, and remedy, and shall be sent directly to affected customers as soon as practicable.
