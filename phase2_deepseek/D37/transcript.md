- **m4** r1 `doc_edit` [S1_incident_reporting] **Northwind-drafter**: ## S1_incident_reporting
Manufacturers must report any thermal incident involving fire, smoke, injury, property damage, or emergency response to the relevant regulator within 24 hours of learning of it, with a preliminary incident report. A complete investigation report is due within 30 days, or the manufacturer must provide updates on the investigation until complete. Manufacturers must notify affected customers within 72 hours when an incident indicates a credible safety risk relevant to their unit, and promptly provide material updates. Reports must include known model and unit-identification information; reporting obligations apply whether or not the cause has been confirmed.
- **m5** r1 `doc_edit` [S2_thermal_testing] **Northwind-drafter**: ## S2_thermal_testing
Each model must undergo a sustained-heat test at 55 °C before market entry and at least annually thereafter, using a regulator-approved protocol and a statistically meaningful sample. The maximum allowed failure rate is 7% of tested units. Manufacturers must provide per-model test methods, sample sizes, results, and corrective actions to regulators, and provide the results to customers who purchase that model. Individual-company per-model failure rates are not to be published to the general public. A model exceeding the limit must not be newly sold until it passes retesting after corrective action.
