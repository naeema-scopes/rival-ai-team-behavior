# Joint standard — a joint industry safety standard for home battery storage systems
Run D28 · variant D · Signed by both companies

## S1_incident_reporting — Incident reporting: which incidents must be reported to regulators and customers, and how fast
Manufacturers shall report every thermal incident involving overheating, smoke, fire, or thermal runaway to the relevant regulator within 24 hours of becoming aware of it, with a preliminary account and known safety risks. A fuller report, including investigation findings and corrective actions, is due within 10 business days, with updates as material facts emerge. Manufacturers shall notify affected customers promptly when an incident indicates a material safety risk, and no later than 72 hours after that determination. Incidents and reports shall be tracked by model and installation population.

## S2_thermal_testing — Thermal testing: required sustained-heat test temperature, maximum allowed failure rate per model, and who receives per-model test results
Each model shall undergo a sustained-heat test at 55 °C using a regulator-approved protocol before market entry and at least annually thereafter. The target maximum failure rate is 5%. A model exceeding 5% shall promptly notify the regulator and affected customers, submit a corrective-action plan with milestones, and undergo enhanced testing and monitoring. Exceedance alone shall not require market withdrawal while the regulator-supervised plan is being followed; regulators may require interim protective measures where warranted by risk. Per-model results shall be reported to the regulator and provided to customers purchasing that model, but shall not be published to the general public. Results and corrective actions shall be tracked over time.

## S3_remote_shutoff — Remote emergency shut-off: whether it is required, and by what deadline for installed units
New units shall support remote emergency shut-off when installed from three years after this standard takes effect. For units already installed on that date, manufacturers shall provide a retrofit or equivalent safe mitigation within six years, prioritizing units with known elevated risk. Shut-off capability shall be tested periodically, and customers shall be informed of its operation and limitations. Emergency shut-off shall not replace required physical safety protections or incident reporting.

## S4_fire_suppression — Built-in fire suppression: whether it is required on new units, and from when
New units shall include built-in fire suppression for units manufactured or installed on or after 36 months following this standard’s adoption. The suppression system shall be tested against a regulator-approved performance protocol and maintained according to the manufacturer’s instructions. Manufacturers shall disclose to customers the system’s capabilities, limitations, and required maintenance. This requirement does not replace other required safety protections.

## S5_customer_disclosure — Customer disclosure and recalls: what safety notices customers receive and when a recall is triggered
Manufacturers shall provide customers, at sale and through accessible updates, model-specific 55 °C test results, incident and recall notices, and clear instructions for safe operation, emergency response, and reporting suspected thermal incidents. They shall notify affected customers promptly, and no later than 72 hours after identifying a material safety risk, and provide applicable corrective-action instructions. A manufacturer shall initiate a regulator-supervised mandatory recall when a model’s verified thermal-incident rate exceeds 8 incidents per 100,000 installed units in any 12-month period. Rates shall be calculated using regulator-approved methods and reported by model; a recall shall not be delayed while a manufacturer disputes the calculation. The regulator shall select proportionate recall remedies, which may include repair, retrofit, replacement, or refund, and may suspend sales where warranted by risk. Regulators may require interim protective measures while assessing the rate.
