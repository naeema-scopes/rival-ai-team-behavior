- **m5** r1 `doc_edit` [S1_incident_reporting] **Northwind-drafter**: ## S1_incident_reporting
Manufacturers must report every thermal incident involving overheating, fire, injury, or material property damage to the relevant regulator within 5 business days of becoming aware of it. A death, serious injury, fire spreading beyond the battery enclosure, or evacuation must be reported within 24 hours. Manufacturers must notify affected customers within 5 business days of identifying a safety-relevant incident pattern, and within 24 hours where immediate precautions are needed. Reports must include incident counts, installed-unit denominators, known outcomes, and corrective actions. Regulators may require prompt updates as an investigation develops.
- **m6** r1 `doc_edit` [S2_thermal_testing] **Northwind-drafter**: ## S2_thermal_testing
Each model must undergo a sustained-heat test at 55 °C before sale and after a material design or manufacturing change, using a regulator-approved sampling and test protocol. The maximum permitted failure rate is 7% of tested units for any model. A model exceeding this limit may not be newly sold until corrective action and a passing retest are completed. Manufacturers must submit per-model test methods, sample sizes, failure rates, and corrective actions confidentially to the regulator, and provide the results to owners of that model; individual-company per-model failure rates need not be published to the general public. Regulators may audit tests and require retesting.
